How to read the sheet

  • Every plaque begins at the exact year its period opened. When two labels would meet, the later one steps into another row — so nothing is ever covered.
  • Some plaques are drawn a little wider than their period so the name stays legible; the dotted gold rule beneath still marks the true span.
  • The scale is compressed through deep antiquity and opened out after 1500, so the crowded modern centuries have room to breathe.
  • A few periods genuinely overlap — the Zhou and the Roman Republic, for instance, were contemporaries for some two hundred and fifty years.
